Step 1 of 3

Start with One Active Change

Choose one change that is currently affecting your team (for example, a new tool or system, a restructure, a process or policy update, new priorities, or a change in leadership). You will use it throughout this course to build your Team Support Plan.

Choose one specific change rather than a general period of transformation.

Step 2 of 3

Check What You Know

Before deciding what to do, separate what you can observe from what you think may be causing it. You do not need complete answers yet.

Observation Possible explanation Question to investigate

1. What can you observe?

Record behavior or events that another person could also see or verify. Avoid labels such as “resistant,” “negative,” or “not committed.”

See an example
ObservationThree people used the old system during the last two high-volume periods.
InterpretationThe team does not support the change.

Only the first statement is directly observable.

2. What might be getting in the way?

Add a possible explanation, but treat it as a hypothesis—not as a fact.

Hypothesis — still needs to be checked

3. What still needs to be checked?

Identify the question, conversation, or evidence that could confirm or challenge your explanation.

You now have an observation, a hypothesis, and a question to investigate. That is enough to choose a useful starting point.
